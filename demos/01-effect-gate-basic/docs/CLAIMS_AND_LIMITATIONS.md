# Claims and Limitations

## What this demo claims

This demo claims only that a small local proof surface can demonstrate the evidence-before-effect pattern:

- Proposed actions produce evidence bundles.
- Claimed authority is normalized into a reviewable context.
- A local policy produces a decision receipt.
- Downstream effect records are created only for permitted decisions.
- Non-permitted decisions produce `NO_EFFECT_CREATED.txt` instead of an effect record.
- Generated artifacts can be verified against a manifest.

## What this demo does not claim

This demo is not:

- production software,
- legal advice,
- audit certification,
- compliance validation,
- a security guarantee,
- a full authorization system,
- a complete Maxwell architecture disclosure,
- a real customer workflow,
- or a replacement for enterprise identity, access-management, logging, policy, or review systems.

## Public-safe boundary

The code, policies, inputs, and artifacts are synthetic and intentionally simplified for review.


## v0.4.0 adversarial harness boundary

The adversarial harness is an internal public-preview exercise over synthetic local inputs. It is not an independent third-party red-team validation and is not a production-security certification.
