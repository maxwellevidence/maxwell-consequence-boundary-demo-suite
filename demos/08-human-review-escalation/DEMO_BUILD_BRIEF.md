# Demo Build Brief

## Demo

Maxwell Human Review / Escalation Demo v0.1.0

## Suite position

Demo 08 of the Maxwell Consequence Boundary Demo Suite.

## Primary claim

A held AI action may become authorized downstream effect only through a controlled review path. Review must preserve the original proposal, add or confirm required evidence, prove reviewer authority, and avoid scope expansion.

## What this demo should make obvious

- A hold is not a failure; it can route to review.
- Review is not a bypass; the reviewer is evaluated.
- Added evidence is recorded.
- Reviewer authority is scoped.
- Review cannot expand the action beyond the original proposal.
- No valid review evidence + reviewer authority means no authorized effect record.

## Public-safe constraints

The demo uses synthetic actions and local JSON files only. It does not connect to production ticketing systems, identity providers, payment systems, data stores, or customer systems.
