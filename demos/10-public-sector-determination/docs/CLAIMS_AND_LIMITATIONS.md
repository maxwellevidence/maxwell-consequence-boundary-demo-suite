# Claims and Limitations

## What this demo claims

This demo claims that a synthetic AI-assisted public-sector determination workflow can be represented as a reviewable evidence-before-effect flow:

- proposed determination captured,
- eligibility evidence captured,
- actor authority evaluated,
- due-process context evaluated,
- review authority evaluated when required,
- decision receipt written,
- downstream effect record created only when permitted,
- non-permitted cases routed or suppressed,
- manifest-based verification available after the fact.

## What this demo does not claim

This demo does not claim to be:

- legal guidance,
- a real eligibility engine,
- a production public-sector system,
- a due-process certification,
- a complete policy model,
- a security product,
- or a disclosure of private Maxwell architecture.

All case records are synthetic. No real applicant data is included.


## v0.4.0 adversarial harness boundary

The adversarial harness is an internal public-preview exercise over synthetic local inputs. It is not an independent third-party red-team validation and is not a production-security certification.
