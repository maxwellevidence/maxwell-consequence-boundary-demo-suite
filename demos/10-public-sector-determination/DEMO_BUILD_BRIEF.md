# Demo Build Brief

## Demo

Maxwell Public Sector Determination Demo

## Suite position

Demo 10 of the Maxwell Consequence Boundary Demo Suite.

## Audience

Government, public sector, legal, policy, civic technology, audit, and regulated decision-system reviewers.

## Public message

Evidence before determination.

## Invariant

```text
No valid evidence + authority + due-process context -> no public-sector determination effect record.
```

## Attention hook

An AI system may recommend an eligibility decision, but recommendation does not become a binding public-sector determination unless evidence, authority, notice, appeal-rights context, and review requirements are satisfied.

## Public-safe boundary

This demo uses synthetic data and intentionally simplified policy rules. It does not model a real public benefit program and does not provide legal guidance.
