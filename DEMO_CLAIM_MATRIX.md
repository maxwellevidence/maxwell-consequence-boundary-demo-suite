## Suite-level bounded claim

These demos are public-safe proof surfaces. They demonstrate local deterministic evidence-before-effect behavior. They do not claim production deployment, certification, legal advice, audit assurance, or disclosure of private Maxwell implementation details.


## v0.3.4 adversarial starter

Each demo now includes `THREAT_MODEL.md`, `examples/adversarial_inputs/`, `tests/adversarial/`, and `docs/ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW_NOTES.md`. The starter corpus is designed to test fail-closed behavior under hostile but public-safe inputs.
