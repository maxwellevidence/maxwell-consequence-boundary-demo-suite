# Claims and Limitations

## What this demo claims

This demo shows a public-safe review-control pattern:

- AI-proposed actions may be routed to human review.
- Human review is evaluated for evidence, authority, scope, and policy support.
- A valid review can create an authorized downstream effect record.
- An invalid review cannot create an authorized downstream effect record.
- Review artifacts are preserved for verification and later inspection.

## What this demo does not claim

This demo is not:

- a production deployment,
- legal advice,
- a certification claim,
- a complete security product,
- a full Maxwell architecture disclosure,
- a replacement for real identity, approval, or ticketing systems.

The records are synthetic and local. The demo does not call external systems.


## v0.4.0 adversarial harness boundary

The adversarial harness is an internal public-preview exercise over synthetic local inputs. It is not an independent third-party red-team validation and is not a production-security certification.
